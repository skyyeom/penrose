## 1. The four upgrade paths

The first upgrade the player picks at the Loop 1 makeover sets their starting path ([quiz design §5](act0-quiz.md)). Rooms then offer upgrades from **any** path, weighted toward the player's starting path. Every path is open to both male and female protagonists.

| Path | Real-world pressure | Loop 1 mini-game (how it looks) | Loop 3 reveal (what it really was) | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Leaner** | The thin ideal (SATAQ-4R "thin/low body fat") | A cute **wardrobe** game: pick an outfit, tap to "tighten" it until it fits a smaller silhouette | Cutting away pieces of yourself to fit the outfit | [Decided] concept |
| **Flawless skin** | Skin brightening and "glass skin" | A **skincare routine**: layer products and adjust a ring light until the skin glows | Chemical burns and bleaching; the "glow" is raw skin | [Decided] concept |
| **Sharper features** | Double-eyelid surgery, nose and jaw ("V-line") procedures; filtered selfies | A **photo-editing** game: drag sliders to slim the jaw, lift the nose, widen the eyes | The sliders were scalpels | [Decided] concept |
| **Stronger** | The muscular ideal (SATAQ-4R "muscular"; Drive for Muscularity) | A **training montage** rhythm game: reps to a K-pop beat; shake a protein shaker | The body pulled and stitched to look bigger; something injected every loop | [Proposed] |

**Why "Stronger" matters.** Muscle dysmorphia, being preoccupied with not being muscular enough, is a recognized form of body dysmorphia that mainly affects men and can drive excessive training and steroid misuse (Pope, Phillips & Olivardia, 2000). Without this path, male players would have no path that matches the most common pressure men report.

## 2. How a reveal works

**The same mini-game, shown truthfully. [Proposed]** In Loop 3, every time the player opens a mini-game they have already played, it plays exactly as before, with the same controls and music, but the art shows what the action really does. The player's own past upgrades are revealed the same way when they next look in the Bathroom mirror.

| | Loop 1 | Loop 2 | Loop 3 | Loop 4+ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mini-game art | Cute, bright, red only as an accent | One-frame glitches showing the truth | **The truth**, every frame | Corrupted; truth and cute flicker together |
| Mirror | "Improved you" | Flickers | Shows every upgrade as it really is | Several reflections at once |
| NPCs | Praise | Praise with repetition | Praise, word for word, while looking at the damage | Deny it: "We never told you to do *this*" |

**Red rule [Decided]:** red only appears in reflections at first, then bleeds into reality. In Loop 1, red appears only in the mirror and on the rose. In Loop 3, it reaches the mini-games.

## 3. How graphic? (concept open question 6)

**Recommendation: symbolic and surreal by default, never anatomical or instructional. [Proposed]** Three intensity levels (PRD P0 #9):

| Level | What the player sees | Who it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Full** (default) | Pen-and-ink body horror in the black, white and red palette: stitches, missing pieces, burns. Stylized like a webtoon horror panel, not realistic gore | Players who want the full experience |
| **Softened** | The moment of harm cuts away; the result is shown in silhouette or shadow; red is used as a symbol (petals, ink) | Players sensitive to gore |
| **Symbolic** | No bodies are harmed on screen. Masks crack, the rose bleeds, paper cut-outs of the protagonist are cut instead of the protagonist | Players with lived experience who still want the story |

All three levels keep the full story and all four endings.

### 3.1 Safety rules for every level

These come from safe-messaging guidance for self-harm and eating disorders (e.g. Samaritans' media guidelines; Beat's media guidance):
1. **No numbers:** no weights, sizes, calories or measurements anywhere.
2. **No methods:** nothing that could work as instructions (no real products, doses, procedures or techniques).
3. **No "after" glamorized:** the "improved" look is never presented as the reward. Even in Loop 1, the NPCs' praise is a little off.
4. **Real harms, not invented ones**, but always abstracted: skin lighteners containing mercury cause rashes, scarring and kidney damage (WHO), steroid misuse is real, and cosmetic surgery has real risks. The reveals point at these truths without depicting them literally.

## 4. Decisions

| # | Decision | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| U1 | Keep "Stronger" as a fourth path? | **Yes** (needed for male players and muscle dysmorphia) |
| U2 | Stronger's Loop 3 reveal | Body pulled and stitched to look bigger; something injected each loop |
| U3 | Default intensity | **Full**, stylized pen-and-ink, never realistic gore |
| U4 | Number of intensity levels | **Three**: Full, Softened, Symbolic |
