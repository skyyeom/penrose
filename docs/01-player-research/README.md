| Survey | 100+ responses | All of the above + the devlog |

**Why about 12 interviews:** in a well-known study, Guest, Bunce & Johnson (2006) found most themes appeared within the first 12 interviews with a fairly similar group. Stop earlier if nothing new is coming up.

**Screener:** 18 or older (Penrose will be rated for mature audiences); has played at least one story-driven game in the last year.

## 3. How to interview well

Follow *The Mom Test* (Fitzpatrick, 2013):
1. **Ask about their life, not your idea.** Don't pitch Penrose until the very end.
2. **Ask about specific past behavior**, not hypotheticals. "Tell me about the last story game you finished," not "Would you buy a game about…?"
3. **Listen more than you talk.** Follow up on emotion: "You said that annoyed you. What happened?"
4. **Write down exact quotes.** They become persona quotes and marketing language.

## 4. Ethics and wellbeing

Penrose's subject is sensitive. Follow these rules for every interview and the survey:
- **Consent:** explain the purpose, that it's voluntary, that participants can skip any question or stop at any time, and how notes are stored.
- **Never ask about eating-disorder or self-harm history**, or for weights, sizes or procedures. Appearance pressure is asked about only in general terms, and is always optional.
- **Support resources** are shared at the end of every interview and survey.
- **Privacy:** store notes under participant IDs (P01, P02…), not names; delete contact details once the research is done.
- **Under 18s are screened out.**

## 5. Analysis

1. Log each interview in the workbook's **Interview log** within 24 hours, tagging each assumption **Supports**, **Contradicts** or leaving it blank.
2. Export the Microsoft Forms responses (**Open in Excel**) and paste them into **Survey responses**.
3. The **Assumptions** sheet counts the evidence for and against each assumption and suggests a status (Validated, Invalidated or Unclear).
4. Group interview quotes into themes (affinity mapping) and write up §6.
5. Update the personas, the canvas and the PRD where an assumption fails.
