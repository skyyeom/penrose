> **What this doc is:** a **Value Proposition Canvas** (Osterwalder, Pigneur, Bernarda & Smith, *Value Proposition Design*, 2014) for each [persona](01-user-personas.md). It matches what each person is trying to do, what bothers them and what they hope for against what Penrose offers, and shows where the fit is weak.
>
> **Adapting the B2B template:** B2B canvases look for value drivers like *increased ROI* or *reduced operational overhead*. Penrose's equivalents: for players, *time well spent*, *an emotional payoff worth talking about* and *feeling safe*; for partners, *views per hour of footage*, *a low-risk port* and *safe to recommend*.

## 1. How to read the canvas

```
        VALUE MAP (Penrose)                    CUSTOMER PROFILE (persona)
 ┌───────────────────────────────┐      ┌───────────────────────────────┐
 │ Gain creators                 │ ───► │ Gains   (what they hope for)  │
 │ Products & services (features)│      │ Jobs    (what they're doing)  │
 │ Pain relievers                │ ───► │ Pains   (what gets in the way)│
 └───────────────────────────────┘      └───────────────────────────────┘
                        FIT = pain relievers and gain creators
                        match the most important pains and gains
```

- **Jobs:** functional (what they do), social (how they want to be seen), emotional (how they want to feel).
- **Pains and gains** are ranked by importance (1 = most important), as the method recommends.
- **Fit:** ✅ addressed · ⚠️ partly addressed · ❌ gap.

## 2. P1 · Alex, the Lore Diver

| Customer profile | | Value map | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Jobs** | Find a story game worth finishing in a weekend · Decode a twist and hidden systems · Discuss theories online (social) · Feel surprised and unsettled (emotional) | **Products & services** | 2–4 h narrative horror · Act 0 quiz · 4 endings · loop and decay · ending debrief · chapter select |
| **Pains** | 1. Choices that change nothing · 2. Twist spoiled before playing · 3. Padding and slow pacing · 4. "Deep" games that are just edgy | **Pain relievers** | Choices decide the ending, and the rose makes it visible · Marketing never shows the reveal · 30–45 min loops with escalating change · Body horror always means something (design principle) |
| **Gains** | 1. A twist that recontextualizes everything · 2. Hidden systems to uncover · 3. Replay value · 4. **Value driver:** *a story that stays with you, and endings worth replaying for.*

## 9. Gaps → actions

| Gap | Action | Goes to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No streamer-safe music | Add a **Streamer mode** setting: original music only (no licensed tracks), Softened intensity preset, hidden debrief details on screen | PRD P1 · #16 Marketing |
| No content-warning card for creators | Add a printable **content-warning card** to the press kit | #16 Marketing |
| Age ratings not planned | Plan **age ratings** (e.g. through IARC for digital storefronts) before any console or mobile pitch | #12 Milestones |
| Cultural accessibility of the English script | Plan a **cultural read-through** of the script by non-Korean readers and Korean readers | #15 Playtest |
| Aesthetic not yet defined | Finish the **moodboard** | #2 |
| Twist protection | Write a **spoiler policy** for all marketing assets | #16 Marketing |
